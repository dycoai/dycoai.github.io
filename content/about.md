## About DyCoAI

**DyCoAI** stands for **Dynamic Collaboration-based AI Applications for Common People**.

DyCoAI is a research and publication site exploring the **dynamic, innovative, and systematic applications of artificial intelligence**, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs) and increasingly capable AI agents, for learning, working, ideas exploration, and other practical activities.

The site focuses on how advances in artificial intelligence can be translated into useful applications for people. Its research and publications explore different application areas, conceptual frameworks, methods, workflows, and emerging forms of human–AI interaction and collaboration. As AI capabilities and application opportunities continue to evolve, DyCoAI will develop multiple research and publication series addressing different practical applications and research questions.

### The First Research and Publication Series: Working with Agentic AI

The first DyCoAI research and publication series is **“Working with Agentic AI: [Specific Occupation Title]”**. Each article focuses on a specific occupation and examines how human skills and agentic skills can be dynamically integrated and coordinated for collaboration across its professional responsibilities.

The series interprets occupational work through **responsibilities, tasks, subtasks, actions, and their skill requirements**, identifying corresponding human and agentic skills and examining how they can be dynamically integrated for collaboration. It also considers task-performance evaluation and how evaluation can support the iterative refinement of existing human and agentic skills and the development or construction of new skills.

This is the **first of multiple DyCoAI research and publication series**. Future research and publications will explore other dynamic and innovative applications of artificial intelligence, particularly LLMs and AI agents, across learning, working, ideas exploration, and other evolving application areas.

## About the Author

**Dr. Mengyao Zhu** holds a PhD in Systems Engineering. His doctoral research investigated the innovative application of **Large Language Models to mathematical learning**, with particular emphasis on how different prompt instructions, reasoning patterns, and persona settings can guide LLM reasoning and improve the quality and reliability of generated mathematical knowledge.

Building on this foundation, **Dr. Zhu's research interests lie in the innovative, practical, and serious applications of Large Language Models and related AI capabilities across different task clusters**, including learning, working, ideas exploration, and other practical activities that present interesting opportunities for research and application.

DyCoAI continues and expands this research direction by developing research and publication series that explore how emerging capabilities of LLMs, Agentic AI, and related artificial-intelligence systems can be transformed into useful, dynamic, and innovative applications for people.
