To operate across websites and online platforms, Web Navigation & Crawling equips an AI agent with the ability to move through pages, follow links, search site structures, monitor content, and interact with publicly accessible digital environments.

It can gather information, track updates, submit forms, or perform permitted web-based operations by interpreting page elements and proceeding from one step to the next.

Its value lies in breadth, speed, and the ability to handle repetitive online exploration across many sources, especially when relevant information is distributed across multiple pages or sites.

However, the skill depends on website availability, access permissions, interface stability, and compliance with terms of service, so results may require validation and human oversight.

In human–AI work, it can support professionals by collecting web-based evidence, monitoring online information, or automating routine browsing tasks while the human defines objectives, constraints, and appropriate use of the collected material.
