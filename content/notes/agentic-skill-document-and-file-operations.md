Managing digital records at scale is the focus of Document & File Operations, an agentic skill that enables an AI agent to create, transform, organize, store, retrieve, rename, move, archive, and otherwise handle documents and files according to defined rules.

It can apply batch operations, convert formats, enforce naming conventions, maintain folder or repository structures, and prepare files for review, analysis, or downstream processing.

Its value lies in consistency, speed, and repeatability when large numbers of records must be processed according to clear procedures.

However, this skill primarily concerns the handling and organization of file objects rather than understanding their meaning or authoring their substantive content, so human review may still be needed for accuracy, relevance, retention decisions, and compliance-sensitive handling.

In human–AI work, it can reduce manual administrative effort or support professionals by keeping document collections structured, traceable, and ready for use.
