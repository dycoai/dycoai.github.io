When an AI agent must compare multiple candidates against explicit criteria, **Scoring & Ranking** operates as an agentic skill that assigns numerical or ordinal values and orders the candidates accordingly.

It can be applied to risks, applications, products, tasks, search results, or other option sets by evaluating each item against defined metrics, weights, thresholds, or rules.

Its value lies in consistent, scalable prioritization, especially when many items must be compared using the same measurable standards.

However, the quality of the ranking depends on the appropriateness of the criteria, the reliability of the input data, and the fairness of the weighting logic.

In human–AI work, the agent can produce an ordered list of candidates, while the human professional reviews the basis for the scores, adjusts for context or judgment, and makes the final selection or decision.
