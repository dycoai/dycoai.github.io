Self-Critique & Uncertainty Reporting represents a critical agentic skill wherein an artificial intelligence system actively evaluates and communicates the reliability of its own generated outputs.

This capability involves systematically identifying and flagging instances of low confidence, inherent methodological limitations, and foundational assumptions embedded within its responses.

By transparently acknowledging the boundaries of its predictive accuracy and the potential margin of error in its conclusions, the system allows human operators to appropriately calibrate their trust and determine when external verification is required.

Ultimately, this introspective mechanism fosters operational transparency, ensuring that the AI clearly distinguishes its internal confidence assessments from both the detailed explanation of its underlying reasoning processes and the objective validation of external facts.
