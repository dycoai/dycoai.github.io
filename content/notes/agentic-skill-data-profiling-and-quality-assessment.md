Operating as a diagnostic agentic skill, Data Profiling & Quality Assessment systematically audits datasets for completeness, statistical distributions, validity constraints, and structural anomalies prior to downstream utilization.

By examining the inherent characteristics of a given data corpus, this agentic skill identifies and reports existing defects, limitations, and fitness-for-use gaps without attempting to repair, clean, or transform the underlying records.

The effectiveness of this automated diagnostic process is measured by the precision of the generated data profile and the thoroughness of the surfaced quality issues, thereby establishing a clear, objective baseline of the data's condition before any corrective preparation or analytical modeling begins.
