For choices that require comparing alternatives, Recommendation & Decision Support functions as an agentic skill that converts evidence, criteria, and constraints into ranked or comparative options with reasons that help a human decision-maker choose among alternatives.

It can synthesize relevant data, evaluate possible courses of action, highlight trade-offs, and present recommendations in a structured, explainable form suitable for professional review.

Its value lies in broadening the set of considered options, making comparison criteria explicit, and reducing the effort needed to assess complex or repetitive choices.

However, recommendations remain decision aids rather than decision substitutes: the human professional retains responsibility for validating assumptions, judging context-specific consequences, and making the final commitment.

In human–AI work, this skill may either provide routine suggestions for low-risk choices or support high-stakes decisions by preparing evidence-based options while leaving authority and accountability with the human.
