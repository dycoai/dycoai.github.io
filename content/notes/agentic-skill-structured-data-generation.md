When an AI agent must transform information into organized, machine-readable forms, **Structured Data Generation** is the agentic skill that produces tables, records, schemas, and datasets according to specified formats.

It can create new structured outputs, convert unstructured or semi-structured inputs into standardized fields, and generate data suitable for storage, analysis, validation, or downstream processing.

Its value lies in consistency, scalability, and format compliance, especially when large volumes of information must be organized repeatedly or according to fixed templates.

However, generated structured data may still require human review for accuracy, completeness, semantic correctness, and appropriateness for the intended professional use.

In human–AI work, this skill may either replace manual data entry and formatting or support a professional who defines the target structure, source material, and quality expectations before the agent executes the generation task.
