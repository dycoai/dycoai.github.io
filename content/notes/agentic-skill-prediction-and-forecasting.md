**Prediction & Forecasting**, as an agentic skill, is the capability of an AI agent to estimate future values, probabilities, trends, or likely outcomes from historical data, current signals, and contextual assumptions.

It applies statistical, probabilistic, or model-based methods to extrapolate patterns, explore possible developments, and quantify uncertainty in ways that support planning, risk assessment, and decision-making.

Its value lies in speed, scale, and consistency when analyzing large or rapidly changing datasets, but forecasts remain conditional on data quality, model assumptions, and the stability of underlying conditions.

In human–AI work, this skill can generate projected scenarios for review or support decisions where the human professional interprets consequences, evaluates risks, and retains accountability for the final judgment.
