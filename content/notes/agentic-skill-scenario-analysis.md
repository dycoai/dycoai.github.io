Operating as a core reasoning and decision-support capability, scenario analysis allows artificial agents to systematically explore "what-if" variants, assess variable sensitivities, and trace consequence chains across multiple hypothetical situations.

Instead of predicting a single definitive outcome or executing formal computational simulations, this function focuses on mapping a spectrum of plausible conditions to reveal how shifting parameters might alter potential results.

By structuring these alternative pathways, the system illuminates underlying risks, uncertainties, and strategic trade-offs, ultimately equipping human operators with the contextual evidence needed to navigate complex, ambiguous environments.
