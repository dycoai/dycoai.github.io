**Bias, Fairness & Policy Monitoring** is an agentic skill that leverages the pattern-recognition and analytical capabilities of foundation models to systematically detect potentially biased, unfair, or policy-violating trends across large-scale datasets, generated outputs, and automated decisions.

Its primary function is to identify systemic disparities and potential ethical deviations, providing value in human–AI collaboration by acting as a continuous, scalable diagnostic layer that augments human oversight and supports efforts toward equitable outcomes without the bottleneck of manual review.

However, this skill is constrained by its reliance on predefined ethical frameworks, explicit policies, underlying data, and model behavior. It cannot autonomously resolve complex societal biases, reliably interpret all nuanced cultural contexts, or make final normative judgments.

Consequently, it operates as an early-warning mechanism within professional workflows, flagging potential ethical risks and policy-compliance concerns so that human professionals can apply contextual judgment, adjudicate edge cases, and retain ultimate accountability for organizational integrity.
