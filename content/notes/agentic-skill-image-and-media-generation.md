When an AI agent must create visual or audiovisual material, **Image & Media Generation** is the **agentic skill** that produces and edits images, illustrations, audio, and video assets from prompts, references, specifications, or existing media.

It can be used to generate concept variants, marketing visuals, illustrations, sound elements, or short video sequences, and to modify style, composition, format, or other attributes while following task constraints.

It is concerned with media assets rather than analytical charts or dashboards, although the two may be combined in a finished presentation.

Its value lies in speed, scale, and the ability to explore many creative options, but the outputs may still require human review for accuracy, appropriateness, originality, brand fit, accessibility, and intended use.

In human–AI work, this skill can either supply draft media for a professional to refine or support routine production tasks, while creative direction, aesthetic judgment, ethical review, and final approval typically remain with the human user.
