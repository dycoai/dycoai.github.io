**Comparison & Matching**, as an agentic skill, is the capability of an AI agent to examine two or more items—such as documents, records, data fields, images, product entries, case files, software versions, or candidate options—and determine how they align, differ, correspond, or duplicate one another.

It typically involves matching equivalent entities across sources, identifying added, removed, or changed elements, detecting near-duplicates, reconciling inconsistent formats, and checking whether versions of the same information remain consistent over time.

The skill is useful when work requires comparing a current record against a prior version, verifying that information from separate sources refers to the same real-world entity, or finding corresponding entries across lists that use slightly different wording, identifiers, or structures.

Its value lies in speed, consistency, and the ability to handle large numbers of pairwise comparisons that would be tedious or error-prone for manual review.

However, comparison and matching usually establish correspondence and difference rather than decide which item is best, assign a final priority, or interpret the broader significance of the result; those later steps often require additional analytical reasoning, domain knowledge, or human judgment.

In human–AI collaboration, this skill can perform the systematic matching and difference detection, while the human professional reviews uncertain matches, resolves ambiguous cases, and decides what the comparison means for the task at hand.
