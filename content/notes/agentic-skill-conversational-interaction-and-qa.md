Operating as a dynamic communicative interface, the agentic skill of Conversational Interaction & Q&A empowers artificial systems to engage in goal-directed dialogue, accurately answer user inquiries, and systematically clarify underlying intents.

Rather than merely retrieving static data, this capability involves parsing natural language inputs, managing the immediate flow of a multi-turn exchange, and resolving ambiguities to ensure precise alignment between user requests and computational responses.

It functions strictly within the boundaries of the active session to facilitate real-time comprehension and interactive problem-solving, distinctly separating the mechanics of immediate conversational engagement from the long-term retention of user history or personalized preferences.
