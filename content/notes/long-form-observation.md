Most occupation-level automation talk jumps straight to "which jobs disappear", but the more useful question is which *tasks* inside a job get re-sequenced. A bioinformatics annotator does not vanish because an agent can draft GO-term annotations; the drafting moves to the agent, and the human moves to evidence adjudication, conflict resolution, and curation standards. That is a different job with the same title, and our categories have not caught up.
