When a task calls for generating possibilities rather than evaluating them, Ideation & Brainstorming Support is the agentic skill through which an AI agent produces options, alternatives, variations, and creative prompts to broaden the solution space.

It can help professionals move beyond initial ideas by proposing multiple directions, reframing problem statements, or supplying starting points for further development.

Its value lies in speed, breadth, and the ability to combine concepts in unconventional ways, but the generated ideas may still require human assessment for relevance, feasibility, originality, and fit with professional goals.

This skill is therefore most useful in early-stage creative, design, research, planning, and problem-solving work, where expanding options is the immediate requirement rather than refining a final message for a particular audience.
