Before data can be analyzed, modeled, or reported reliably, it often must be made consistent and usable, and Data Preparation & Feature Transformation is the agentic skill that performs this work.

It enables an AI agent to clean, normalize, join, reshape, deduplicate, encode, scale, and derive features from raw or partially structured inputs so that the resulting dataset is ready for analysis, machine learning, visualization, or downstream processing.

Its value lies in speed, repeatability, and scalability, especially when large or heterogeneous datasets require standardized preprocessing steps that would be time-consuming to perform manually.

However, the skill is constrained by the quality and meaning of source data, the correctness of transformation rules, and the risk that inappropriate cleaning, joining, or feature construction may introduce errors, bias, or misleading representations.

In human–AI collaboration, the agent can execute routine preparation pipelines and propose transformations, while the human professional defines data requirements, validates semantic correctness, reviews edge cases, and remains responsible for the suitability of the prepared data for its intended use.
