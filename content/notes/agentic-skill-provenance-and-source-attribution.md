**Provenance & Source Attribution** is an agentic skill that leverages the foundational capabilities of AI models to systematically track, document, and report the sources and origins of facts, data, and generated content through precise citations and traceability markers.

Its primary value lies in establishing transparency, accountability, and trust in automated outputs, which is essential for mitigating the impact of hallucinations and enabling human professionals to confidently validate information accuracy within their specific occupational responsibilities.

However, the execution of this skill is constrained by the model's context and retrieval limitations, its reliance on accessible or indexed data, and the need for external tool integrations to access restricted or proprietary sources. It also operates within a strict boundary of tracing factual origins rather than exposing the underlying reasoning process of the AI's conclusions.

In the context of human–AI collaboration, it serves as a vital governance mechanism that empowers the human expert to maintain rigorous oversight, verify automated synthesis, and make defensible, evidence-based decisions by bridging the gap between machine-generated information and human validation requirements.
