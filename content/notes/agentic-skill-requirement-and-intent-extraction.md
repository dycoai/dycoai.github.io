Requirement & Intent Extraction gives an AI agent the ability to identify what stakeholders are asking for, what they need, and what they intend, even when those points are only partially stated.

It can examine documents, records, meeting notes, tickets, or dialogue to surface explicit requirements, implicit expectations, goals, constraints, priorities, and acceptance conditions that should guide later work.

Its value lies in reducing ambiguity, revealing overlooked needs, and converting scattered or informal language into a clearer basis for planning, design, analysis, or compliance.

However, this skill goes beyond copying literal facts or fields: it must interpret meaning, context, and unstated implications, which can introduce errors if the source is vague, conflicting, or domain-specific.

In human–AI work, the agent can propose candidate requirements and intents for review, while the professional validates interpretations, resolves ambiguities, and confirms what truly matters for the task.
