Serving as a systematic engine for investigative inquiry, **Hypothesis Generation & Testing** is an agentic skill that formulates testable propositions from incomplete evidence and designs or conducts discriminating checks to confirm or refute them.

It functions by synthesizing patterns, anomalies, and domain context into candidate explanations, then proposing or running targeted experiments, queries, or simulations that distinguish among competing possibilities.

Its value lies in accelerating the early stages of diagnosis, research, and problem-solving by expanding the set of plausible explanations and reducing the time needed to identify viable lines of inquiry.

However, this skill is constrained by the quality and scope of available data, the risk of generating spurious or untestable hypotheses, and its inability to substitute for professional judgment in interpreting ambiguous results or making consequential judgments.

In human–AI collaboration, the agent typically generates and preliminarily tests hypotheses at scale, while the human professional evaluates their plausibility, designs definitive validation strategies, and retains accountability for final interpretations and decisions.
