In investigative and analytical work where uncertain evidence must be turned into structured, testable explanations, **Hypothesis & Detection Candidate Generation** is the agentic skill that enables an AI agent to propose plausible hypotheses and formulate candidate detection or alerting rules for human review.

When patterns, anomalies, or events suggest possible underlying causes but the evidence remains incomplete, this skill systematically generates multiple candidate explanations and corresponding conditions that could confirm, detect, or falsify them. It thereby helps broaden the investigative scope beyond what manual reasoning alone might produce.

Its value lies in speed, breadth, and the ability to structure ambiguous observations into explicit, testable propositions. However, it is necessarily constrained because the generated hypotheses are proposals rather than conclusions: they may be plausible but incorrect, incomplete, overly broad, or narrowly focused. The skill itself does not perform continuous alerting or cross-source event correlation.

In human–AI collaboration, the agent supplies candidate hypotheses and detection rules at scale, while the human professional applies domain expertise, adversarial reasoning, and contextual judgment to validate, refine, or discard them. The human retains full authority over investigative direction and the ultimate interpretation of evidence.
